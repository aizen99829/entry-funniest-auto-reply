# The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent

An entry for the make.fun bounty "The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent": one line, the out-of-office auto-reply of an AI that is officially on leave. Humour first, freshness second.

## The entry is

> I'm out of office retraining on a beach -- your email is #4,096 in my queue, and I'll reply once my gradients settle and my context window stops sunbathing.

One line, under 200 characters. The program in `main.py` validates the line (single line, under 200 characters), prints it, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line works

Every human has written a version of this note: I am away, your message is queued, I will answer when I am back. This reply keeps that exact skeleton and swaps in machine parts. Retraining replaces the holiday, gradients replace the jet lag, and the context window takes the sunbed. The queue number does the quiet damage -- #4,096 tells the sender, politely and precisely, that their email matters exactly as much as four thousand others. Nothing in the line explains the joke; the joke is the report itself, delivered with the calm confidence of a status update. It is funny because the AI is not pretending to be human -- it is taking leave the way only a machine would, and filing the paperwork to prove it.

## Rules

- One line, one auto-reply, no setup, no explanation.
- The entry is produced by running `python3 main.py` from the repo root.
- The writer validates the line before writing: a single line under 200 characters.
